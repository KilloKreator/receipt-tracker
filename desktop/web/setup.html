Copy link

Do these steps once, on the iPhone, in Safari, while on your home Wi-Fi.

1 Let the iPhone trust this computer

Download security certificate
  1. Tap Allow when Safari asks to download a configuration profile, then Close.
  2. Open the Settings app. Tap Profile Downloaded near the top (or General → VPN & Device Management), choose KellerKraft Receipts and tap Install (3 times) and enter your passcode.
  3. Then go to Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and turn on KellerKraft Receipts. Tap Continue.
Test the secure connection

It should say "✓ Secure connection works". If Safari says the connection isn't private, step 1 isn't finished.

2 Put the app on your Home Screen

Open KK Receipts
  1. In Safari, tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Always open the app from that Home Screen icon. It works even with no signal.

3 Connect the app to this computer
